## Before the session

Read the week 7 lecture before you arrive — the studio spends its time
applying the security-versus-signifier lens to redesigns already in
progress, not introducing it from scratch.

## In the session

Studio time continues, now with the trust lens from the lecture layered onto
the access lens from week 5. A redesign that nails one and ignores the other
is flagged early here rather than at the crit. Bring drawings or annotated
photos, not just prose — the proposal brief requires them, and this is the
session to find out whether yours actually show the thing.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. The redesign crit is next week, then the proposal is due.
