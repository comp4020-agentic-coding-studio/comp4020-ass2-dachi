## Before the session

Read the week 5 lecture. Bring your marked-up audit — you'll be rereading it
through a lens the audit itself didn't ask for.

## In the session

Callum leads a walk-through of campus doors most of the room has walked
through a hundred times without noticing what they demand: door weight,
approach clearance, threshold height, timing on an automatic opener. The
studio re-examines one door each person already audited, this time asking who
it was built for by default.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. This session sets up the access half of the redesign
proposal, due at week 8.
