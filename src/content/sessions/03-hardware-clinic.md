## Before the session

Read the week 3 lecture first — the clinic assumes the vocabulary it sets up
and spends its time on hardware in hand instead of on slides.

## In the session

Callum brings a working set of hardware salvaged from campus maintenance:
hinges of three types, closers set to different speeds and forces, latches,
strikes, and the panic bar off a fire door. Everyone gets to adjust a closer
and feel what "too fast" and "too much force" mean in the hand, not just on
paper. The last half hour is spent finalising the ten doors for the audit and
checking, out loud, that the group's set doesn't repeat a corridor.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. The audit itself is due at week 4.
