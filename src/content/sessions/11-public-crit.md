## Before the session

Bring the finished prototype and the one-page test script. This is the
session the prototype and crit assessment is due in.

## In the session

The panel is deliberately made up of people who only read the redesign
proposal, not the thinking behind it since — the same gap between a document
and the person meeting it cold that the real door will face. Each prototype
is handed to someone who hasn't touched it before; if they need you to
narrate what to do, that's noted as a finding, not smoothed over.

## Afterwards

The prototype and crit is marked from this session, worth 25% of the course.
