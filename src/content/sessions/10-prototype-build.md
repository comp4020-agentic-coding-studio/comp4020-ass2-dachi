## Before the session

Bring whatever you've built so far, however unfinished, plus the materials
you need to keep going.

## In the session

Working time, mostly. Ask during the studio first — most hardware questions
are faster answered with the door in front of you than by email, and this
session is built around that. Partway through, everyone tests their
in-progress prototype on someone else in the room, cold, with no coaching —
the same test the public crit will run, at lower stakes.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. The public crit, where the finished prototype is defended,
is next week.
