## Before the session

Read the week 12 lecture and bring a rough draft of your field guide's
categories, even if the worked examples aren't finished yet.

## In the session

The last lecture widens the lens past this semester's own doors; this closing
crit spends the studio testing whether each person's draft typology survives
that wider view. Petra runs the room the same way as week 1's first door
session, but backwards: instead of building shared vocabulary from scratch,
the studio pressure-tests whether everyone's own categories actually
generalise, including onto a door nobody in the course has audited.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted here. The field guide itself is due at the end of the
teaching period, worth 30% of the course — the last of the four assessments.
