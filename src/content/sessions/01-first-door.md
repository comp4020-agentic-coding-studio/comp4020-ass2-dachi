## Before the session

Bring the door you just walked through. Not a photo from the internet, not a
door you remember fondly — the one you used today, still fresh enough that
you can describe what you did with your hands without having to think about
it.

## In the session

Petra opens with the semester's shape: twelve weeks, four assessments, one
object. Then the room works through everyone's front door together, one at a
time, asking the same three questions of each: what does it want you to do,
what did you actually do, and were those the same thing. The point isn't to
find fault this early — it's to notice how rarely anyone has looked this
closely at a door before.

Studio time closes with a short list-making exercise: everyone leaves with
three candidate doors for their audit, chosen for how different they are from
each other, not how annoying they are.

## Afterwards

No mark this week. What leaves is a shortlist and a shared vocabulary the
rest of the semester builds on.
