## Before the session

Reread your own audit entry for the door you're about to pick. The proposal
brief is explicit that the diagnosis has to survive unchanged from the audit
— this session is where that gets checked early, while it's still cheap to
fix.

## In the session

Working studio time, not a lecture. Petra circulates while people sketch
first fixes for their chosen door, pushing back hard on anyone quietly
redesigning an easier problem than the one their audit actually found. Pick
your worst door, not your funniest one, is said out loud more than once.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. The redesign proposal is due at week 8, after one more
studio session and a crit.
