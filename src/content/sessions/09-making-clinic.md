## Before the session

Read the week 9 lecture and bring your marked-up redesign proposal — the
clinic is spent matching methods to your specific fix, not surveying methods
in the abstract.

## In the session

Callum runs this one like the hardware clinic in week 3, but pointed forward:
cardboard and foamcore mockups, laser-cut signage, small 3D-printed parts, and
where a working model at reduced scale is more honest than a full-size one
you don't have the budget or time for. Everyone leaves having named the one
part of their redesign that's genuinely hard to prototype, rather than
discovering it in week 10.

## Afterwards

Nothing submitted. Prototype build time starts next week.
