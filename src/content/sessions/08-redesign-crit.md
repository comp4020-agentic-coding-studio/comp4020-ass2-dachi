## Before the session

Bring a near-final draft of your redesign proposal — drawings included. This
crit is the last check before it's marked, not a first look.

## In the session

Both Petra and Callum sit the panel this week: access and trust, the two
lenses the proposal has to hold at once, each get a voice. Presentations are
short and deliberately interrupted — a real crit, not a rehearsed pitch —
because a proposal that only survives an uninterrupted run-through hasn't
been tested yet.

## Afterwards

The redesign proposal is submitted by the end of this session, worth 25% of
the course.
