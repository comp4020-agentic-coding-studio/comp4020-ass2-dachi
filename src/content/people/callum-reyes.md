Callum audits buildings for accessibility compliance for a living, which
mostly means measuring door hardware against a code that treats "usable by
someone with one working hand, or in a hurry, or under 140cm tall" as a floor,
not an edge case. He runs the accessibility walk-through in week 5 and the
hardware clinic in weeks 3 and 9, and marks the Door Audit and the Redesign
Proposal.

He is the person to ask when your redesign is elegant and would fail an
inspection, which happens to almost everyone at least once.
