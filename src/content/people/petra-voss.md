Petra spent eleven years as a working architect before noticing that every
building review she sat in argued for hours about facades and roof lines and
never once about the door someone would actually touch first. Doorology is the
course she wishes had existed instead of a semester of studio crits about
buildings nobody would ever occupy.

She sets the semester's studio programme, runs the opening and closing crits,
and marks the Field Guide. If a door is doing something inexplicable and you
can't work out whether that's a design decision or a mistake, that's the
question she wants to hear.
