# Assessment

Four assessments carry the whole course, weighted to sum to exactly 100%,
and each grows out of the one before it: the audit finds a door worth
fixing, the redesign proposes the fix, the prototype builds and defends it,
and the field guide turns what all three taught you into a typology someone
else can use on a door they've never seen.
