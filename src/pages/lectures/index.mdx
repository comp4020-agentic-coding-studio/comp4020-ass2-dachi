# Lectures

Doorology runs on six lectures and twelve studio sessions, not the other way
round. A lecture hands you vocabulary and cases you couldn't get from your
own doors alone — affordance and signifier, security theatre, prototyping
fidelity — and the studio sessions that follow put it to work.
