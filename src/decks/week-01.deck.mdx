# Doorology

Twelve weeks. One object.

---

## You have already used a few hundred doors

Nobody taught you how.

That gap is the course.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "Find ten doors. For each one, say what it wants you to do, what you
> actually did the first time, and whether those were the same thing."

— the door audit brief

---

## Four assessments, one object

- **Door audit** (20%) — ten real doors, judged
- **Redesign proposal** (25%) — fix one, on paper
- **Prototype and crit** (25%) — build it, defend it
- **Field guide** (30%) — a typology someone else can use

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Why doors

Small enough to hold in your head completely.

Common enough that you stopped noticing them years ago.

---

## The shape of the semester

1. Look properly (weeks 1–4): audit
2. Fix one thing (weeks 5–8): redesign
3. Make it real (weeks 9–11): prototype
4. Generalise (week 12): field guide

---

## What a studio is, that a lecture isn't

This is one of six lectures all semester.

Everything else is studio: crits, clinics, working time.

You will spend more time in front of doors than in front of slides.
